# FUTURE_DS_03 – Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes marketing campaign and customer conversion data to understand funnel performance and conversion behavior.

The objective is to identify:
- Customer conversion trends
- High-performing customer segments
- Campaign performance patterns
- Opportunities to improve conversions

---

## Dataset Used
Bank Marketing Campaign Dataset

---

## Tools Used
- Excel
- Data Analytics
- Business Intelligence
- Marketing Funnel Analysis

---

## Key Analysis Performed
- Conversion Rate Analysis
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Contact Method Analysis
- Monthly Conversion Trend Analysis
- Education Level Conversion Analysis

---

## Key Insights
- Certain customer job segments showed higher conversion rates.
- Communication method influenced customer response behavior.
- Conversion rates varied across different campaign periods.
- Education level affected customer engagement and conversions.

---

## Business Recommendations
- Focus campaigns on high-converting customer groups.
- Improve communication strategies for low-performing segments.
- Launch campaigns during high-performing periods.
- Use customer segmentation for targeted marketing campaigns.
